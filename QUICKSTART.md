# Quick start: get your sign-up link

1. Open <https://sheets.new>. Click the title and rename it **Soup Cook Off Sign-Ups**.
2. Click **Extensions** > **Apps Script**. A new tab opens with a code box.
3. Click inside the code box, press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac) to select all, press Delete.
4. Open `apps-script/SoupCookOff.gs` from this repo, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C to copy.
5. Back in the code box, press Ctrl+V to paste, then Ctrl+S to save.
6. Click the blue **Deploy** button (top right) > **New deployment**.
7. Click the gear next to "Select type" > **Web app**.
8. Set **Execute as** to **Me** and **Who has access** to **Anyone**. Click **Deploy**.
9. Click **Authorize access**, pick your Google account. If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click **Advanced**, then **Go to ... (unsafe)**, then **Allow**.
10. Copy the **Web app URL** (ends in `/exec`). That is your link.

Then: open the link in a private window to see what cooks see. In your sheet, the **Organizers** tab has your passcode. Tap "Organizer sign-in" at the bottom of the page to use it.
